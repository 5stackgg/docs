# Features

5Stack is a comprehensive Counter-Strike management system, servers,
matchmaking, tournaments, leagues, stats, and everything you do with a match
after it ends. This is the full picture of what the platform offers.

Pages linked below go deeper on the bigger features.

## Play

- **[Play & Matchmaking](/features/quick-play)**: one Play page with a mode
  picker and **Find Match** for Competitive, Wingman, Duel, and Rush, with
  regions you star and cap by ping, LAN or online, party queueing, your
  schedule, and every other way into a game.
- **[Draft Lobbies](/features/draft-lobbies)**: host a pick-up game in one click
  or with every setting: auto-split, captains draft, host-assigned, or pre-made
  teams, with a pick clock, the whole pick order on screen, benches, and a
  substitute waitlist.
- **[Matches](/features/matches)**: one-page match creation, server selection
  (on-demand, dedicated, or third-party), map/region veto on one board with
  [veto scouting](/features/map-veto#veto-scouting) (each map's edge, both
  sides' records, the other side's usual bans and picks), enforced check-in
  with auto-cancel, full match rules (knife, overtime, pauses, best-of, coaches,
  and more), turn reminders on any page, and a guided admin dock for organizers.
- **[Scrim Finder](/features/scrim-finder)**: advertise when your team plays,
  set who you'll play, and negotiate scrims without a Discord thread.
- **[Player Cameras](/features/player-cameras)**: require a webcam from every
  player, watch the feeds from the panel, auto-pause when one drops, and put the
  spectated player's camera on the broadcast.
- **[Utility Library](/features/utility-library)**: a lineup library for every
  map, built from throws recorded in game and grenades mined from your own
  demos, with filmed preview clips and stills, the smoke the game itself
  simulated, team executes timed to land together, and **Discover** to find new
  smoke throws onto any spot and prove them on a real server.
- **[Practice Servers](/features/practice-servers)**: one-click CS2 practice
  servers driven from an in-game menu and HUD, with scored drills, mastery,
  executes, and a ranked plan of what to learn next.
- **Custom Map Pools**: one pool per format (1v1, 2v2, 3v3 Rush, 5v5),
  including workshop maps.

## Compete

- **[Tournaments & Brackets](/features/tournaments)**: open, invite-only, or
  rank-gated tournaments with check-in and a free-agent pool drafted into
  balanced teams; multi-stage Single/Double Elimination, Round Robin, or Swiss
  brackets; owners entering several teams; negotiated match times, a stats
  leaderboard, an explained or organizer-picked MVP, a podium even with awards
  off, and a live, embeddable bracket.
- **[Leagues](/features/leagues)**: seasonal divisions on the tournament engine,
  weekly match play, captain-negotiated scheduling, playoffs, and
  promotion/relegation.
- **[Events](/features/events)**: wrap a LAN or a cup weekend into one page that
  follows the event before, during, and after, with a medal table, leaderboard,
  match list, highlights, and a media gallery with a lightbox.
- **[Competitive Seasons](/features/seasons)**: periodic Elo resets with
  season-scoped stats and leaderboards, season awards (Champion to MVP), and
  season pace, compare, and dividers on player profiles.

## Match review & media

- **[Match Analysis](/features/match-analysis)**: scoreboard lenses, a
  start-of-round economy chart and buy-type breakdown, clutches, head-to-head,
  detected roles, heatmaps and movement paths, plus team veto tendencies, a
  veto simulator, and live veto scouting on the veto board.
- **[Match Replay (2D & 3D)](/features/match-replay)**: replay any map on a stage
  drawn in your browser, top-down or in 3D, with CS2's real smokes, every
  bullet, fog of war, an auto-director camera, shareable moments, and clip
  export.
- **[In-Browser Demo Playback](/features/demo-playback)**: the real demo
  rendered on a GPU and streamed to your browser, with player POV switching,
  X-ray, a kill-marked timeline, and your broadcast HUD.
- **[Highlights](/features/highlights)**: automatic best-round reels for every
  player, top plays on the Watch page, a theater-mode clip player, and an
  in-app clip builder.
- **[Live Streaming](/features/live-streaming)**: a Watch page with a match
  ticker and live stage, low-latency WebRTC streams, the Stream Deck, broadcast
  HUDs from your own library, players' Twitch POVs, and a floating player.
- **[Demos](/features/demos)**: automatic upload, fast parsing, storage,
  retention, download, and reparsing.

## Stats

- **[Stats & Leaderboards](/features/stats-and-leaderboards)**: HLTV-style
  ratings, KAST/ADR, opening duels, clutches, roles, and Elo on tabbed player
  profiles; expand any match into the full lobby; compare against another
  player or your own past season; global leaderboards plus FACEIT and Premier
  boards for your community.
- **[Performance Rating](/features/performance-rating)**: Aim, Positioning, and
  Utility scored out of 100 against every other player, or against one season's
  pool, with focus areas and coaching notes.
- **[External Matches](/features/external-matches)**: import your Valve and
  FACEIT matches, paste a single share code, or upload a demo to get full stats,
  replay, and highlights.

## Community

- **[Friends, Teams & the Hub](/features/social)**: the floating hub dock
  (Inbox, Party, Voice, Friends, Chat, Matches), Steam friend sync with live
  presence, teams, parties, blocking, and linked Discord and Twitch accounts
  (live on profiles, in search, and as match POVs).
- **[Chat](/features/chat)**: match, permanent team, lobby, draft, tournament,
  and organizer rooms plus direct messages, with @mentions, per-room mute and
  notify levels, scroll-back history, reactions, 10-minute edits, image and
  video attachments, GIPHY GIFs, message requests, in-game all/team chat relay,
  and a post-match chat log.
- **[Voice Chat](/features/voice-chat)**: party and team voice on 5Stack's own
  relay, with a Voice app on the dock, per-player volume, a mic check, and
  voice-activity transmission.
- **[Steam Presence](/features/steam-presence)**: see what friends are playing,
  in 5Stack and outside it.
- **[Moderation & Sanctions](/features/moderation)**: bans, mutes, gags,
  silences, and warnings that follow a player everywhere, chat moderation,
  automatic VAC bans, escalating sanctions for abandons and tournament
  no-shows, and actions only on players below your own role.
- **[News](/features/news)**: first-party announcements written and published
  from inside the panel, with comments, +1s, and reply notifications.
- **Discord Integration**: create matches from a Discord voice channel, run the
  map veto, and get per-team voice channels with the 5Stack bot.

## Servers & infrastructure

- **Flexible Server Management**: on-demand servers, dedicated servers on your
  own nodes, or external machines, all from one panel.
- **[Community Servers](/servers/dedicated-servers/community-servers)**: public
  servers with live sanction enforcement, private access lists, map rotation
  from Steam Workshop collections, per-server plugins, and per-server stats and
  leaderboards.
- **Move servers between nodes**: a dedicated server's files travel with it,
  with progress and automatic rollback.
- **Automatic CS2 updates**: servers stay on the latest CS2 build (and 5Stack
  plugin) automatically, with optional version pinning when you need stability,
  and failed updates are flagged with their logs.
- **Regional Servers**: multi-region and LAN-capable, with built-in Steam Relay
  to hide your real server IPs.
- **Server Insights**: live CPU / memory / disk, logs, crash reports, CPU
  pinning, low-latency kernel status, and offline alerts that don't cry wolf.
- **[GPU Nodes](/servers/gpu-nodes/)**: power highlights, demo playback, live
  streaming, and utility lineup previews.

## Platform

- **[Notifications](/features/notifications)**: one Inbox and one settings page
  for every kind of notification, with per-kind bell switches, per-group push,
  quiet hours, per-room chat mutes, @mention pushes, urgent match-found rings,
  and a flashing browser tab.
- **Installable app (PWA)**: add 5Stack to your home screen or desktop for an
  app-like experience, with offline-aware caching and an unread badge on the
  app icon.
- **Built for phones**: the hub slides in over the page, matchmaking settings
  open as swipe-to-close sheets, and Back returns lists to where you left them.
- **[Push notifications](/advanced/web-push)**: per-category, per-device alerts
  that reach players when the app isn't open, with no third-party push service to
  sign up for.
- **[17 languages](/features/languages)**: the interface *and* the in-game
  plugin are fully localized, with automatic detection of your browser language.
- **Embeds & popouts**: drop a live, auto-cycling tournament bracket onto a
  stream or site, and pop replays and broadcasts out into their own windows for a
  second monitor.
- **Search**: fast player and entity search from anywhere in the app.
- **[API & personal keys](/features/api-keys)**: generate personal API keys for
  programmatic access to your data.
- **[5Stack Plugins](/plugins/)**: embed your own web applications as native
  panel pages, sharing the panel's session and design system. (For CS2
  server-side plugins, see
  [Game Plugins](/servers/game-server-nodes/plugin-runtimes).)

## For administrators

5Stack gives admins deep control over how the platform behaves:

- **[Administration Overview](/features/admin/)**: roles and access control.
- **[Roles & Players](/features/admin/roles-and-players)**: manage roles,
  the player directory, and sanctions.
- **[Matchmaking & Ranks](/features/admin/matchmaking)**: match types, Elo
  ranks, lineups, map pools, and per–game-type configs.
- **[Branding & Theming](/features/admin/branding)**: name, logo, login page,
  and the color theme.
- **[Servers & Regions](/features/admin/servers)**: server types, automatic CS2
  updates and version pinning, match server controls, community servers, moving
  servers between nodes, tuning, and regions.
- **[Game Modes](/features/admin/game-modes)**: bundle game plugins, cvars, and
  a Valve game mode into unranked modes for matches, draft lobbies, and
  community servers.
- **[Integrations](/features/admin/integrations)**: Discord, Twitch, external
  match imports, analytics, and chat.
- **[System & Monitoring](/features/admin/system)**: metrics, logs, database
  performance, backups, and the render queue.

Several features are optional and can be switched on or off instance-wide from
**Settings → Application**: matchmaking, leagues, seasons, events, news, the
Scrim Finder, voice chat, plugins, and linked accounts.

::: tip Rendering features need a GPU
Highlights, in-browser demo playback, live streaming, and utility lineup
previews are all rendered on a **GPU node**. See
[GPU Nodes](/servers/gpu-nodes/). The replay stage and Discover run without one.
:::
