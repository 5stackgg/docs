# Languages

5Stack speaks your language. The interface is fully localized in **15+
languages** — including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese
(Brazil), Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Danish, Swedish, Arabic, Japanese,
Korean, and Chinese (Simplified & Traditional).

5Stack **auto-detects** your browser language on first visit, and you can switch
languages any time — your choice is remembered.

## Help translate

Translations are community-driven. If your language is missing something, you can
help improve it — see [Localization](/community/localization/) to contribute.
