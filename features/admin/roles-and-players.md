# Roles & Players

The player directory is where admins find players, manage their roles, and apply
restrictions. For how roles map to permissions, see the
[role hierarchy](/features/admin/#roles-access-control).

## Player directory

Search by name and narrow the list with the advanced filters:

- **Role** — filter by a specific role.
- **Elo range** — slider.
- **Country**, and **country-ban status**.

The list shows each player's avatar, name, Elo, roles, status, and last-active
time.

## Managing roles

Open a player to change their role. Promote trusted players to **Verified User**,
**Streamer**, **Match Organizer**, **Tournament Organizer**, or **Administrator**
as needed — remember that most features are gated by a **minimum role**, so
promoting a player unlocks every feature at or below their new role.

## Sanctions

Players can be sanctioned with **bans, mutes, gags, and silences**, which sync
across matches and servers. Matchmaking and streaming can also be restricted by
role.

## Maintenance

You can queue a full **refresh of all players'** data from Steam at once.

## Organizers chat

**Match Organizers** and above get an **organizers-only chat** room for
coordinating events out of public view.
