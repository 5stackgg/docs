# Branding & Theming

Branding (administrator only) lets you make the panel your own — name, logos,
login page, and a full light/dark color theme.

## General

- **Brand name** — shown throughout the panel.
- **Border radius** — global corner rounding (0–1.5rem).
- **Show separators** — toggle UI divider lines.

## Assets

- **Logo** — PNG, JPEG, SVG, or WebP.
- **Favicon** — PNG, JPEG, ICO, or WebP.

Each has a preview and a remove button.

## Login page

- **Show login footer** — toggle.
- **Footer text** and **Footer URL**.

## Theme colors

Light and dark modes are themed independently. Each mode exposes:

- **Core colors** — background, foreground, primary, secondary, accent, muted,
  destructive, warning.
- **Cards & borders** — card, border, popover, input, ring.
- **Sidebar** — background, foreground, accent, border, primary, ring.
- **Top nav** — background, foreground, accent, border, primary, ring.
- **Tactical amber** — the accent used for highlights and live indicators.

## Saving & sharing themes

- **Save** to apply, or **Reset to defaults**.
- **Export theme** to a JSON file and **Import theme** from one — handy for moving
  a look between instances or sharing it.
