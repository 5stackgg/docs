# Branding & Theming

Branding (administrator only) lets you make the panel your own: name, logos,
login page, and the color theme. 5Stack has a single dark theme, so every color
you set applies everywhere.

Find it under **Settings → Application → Branding**. Color changes preview live,
and nothing is saved until you press **Save changes** in the **Unsaved
changes** bar that appears.

## General

- **Brand Name**: shown in the sidebar and the page title.
- **Border Radius**: global corner rounding for buttons, cards and inputs
  (0–1.5rem).
- **Show Separators**: toggle the divider lines between sections.

## Logo & Favicon

- **Logo**: one square image (512×512 PNG recommended; PNG, JPEG, SVG or WebP).
  It's used across the app and to generate your PWA install icon.
- **Favicon**: optional, a simpler mark for the browser tab (PNG, JPEG, ICO or
  WebP).

Each has a preview and a remove button.

## Login Page

- **Show Login Footer**: toggle.
- **Footer Text** and **Footer URL**.

## Theme Colors

- **Core**: background, foreground, primary, secondary, accent, muted,
  destructive, warning, each with its foreground.
- **Cards & Borders**: card, border, popover, input, focus ring.
- **Sidebar**: background, text, active/hover, border, primary, focus ring.
- **Top Nav**: background, text, hover, border, primary, focus ring.
- **Tactical**: the **Tactical Accent (amber highlights)**, used for highlights,
  live indicators, the open app on the hub dock, and your team in a match's
  header and stat tables.

::: tip There is no light mode
5Stack used to carry a light palette that nothing displayed. It was removed, so
there's one set of colors to brand rather than two.
:::

## Saving & sharing themes

- **Export Theme** to a JSON file and **Import Theme** from one, handy for moving
  a look between instances or sharing it.
- **Reset to Defaults** clears your brand name, colors, login footer, and
  uploaded logo and favicon, restoring the default 5Stack theme. It can't be
  undone.
