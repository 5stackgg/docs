# Browser Extension Privacy Policy

This policy covers the **5Stack** browser extension.

## What it reads

Only when you click its icon, and only on FACEIT and Leetify pages:

- the match or player on the page you are looking at;
- the matches FACEIT and Leetify list for it: match ids, maps, scores, dates,
  and Valve share codes;
- when you press **Import** on a FACEIT match, a download link for its demo,
  which FACEIT gives to your FACEIT login.

## Where it goes

Only to the 5Stack panel you set up in the extension, which is 5stack.gg unless
you typed another address. The extension sends that panel the match ids, share
codes and demo links of the matches you choose to import, along with your
panel's own login cookie so the panel knows who is importing.

It sends nothing to 5Stack's developers or anyone else, and it has no
analytics.

## What it stores

The address of your 5Stack panel, in your browser. It never reads, stores or
sends your FACEIT or 5Stack login itself: those stay in your browser's cookies
and are only used by FACEIT's and your panel's own sites.

## Your panel

What happens to an imported match is up to the panel you import into. For
5stack.gg, imported matches are visible like any other external match. A
self-hosted panel is run by its own operator.
