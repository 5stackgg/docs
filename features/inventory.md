# Inventory & Sticker Crafts

The [5Stack Inventory](https://github.com/lukepolo/5stack-inventory-plugin) is
a [panel plugin](/plugins/): a CS2 inventory and loadout simulator that runs
inside your panel. Players craft and equip skins, knives, gloves, agents,
stickers and charms, and see them in game on your servers. Once installed it
shows as **Inventory** in the navigation; see the plugin's README for
installing it and putting skins on your game servers.

## Sticker Crafts

**Sticker Crafts** lays out one gun's worth of stickers once, so you can put
the same combo on any gun. Sticker Crafts was built by community member
Kristians Laukis (LCrew).

### The mat

The **Sticker Crafts** tab is a 3D cutting mat ruled in centimetres. Add up to
five stickers and arrange them:

- Drag a sticker to move it, and **Shift**-drag to turn it. **Q** and **E** turn
  it 5°, and the arrow keys nudge it.
- **Snap** holds half centimetres and 5° turns.
- Pick a gun from the **Outline** rail to chalk its side on the mat, with each
  sticker slot dashed and numbered. The mat and its rulers resize to that gun's
  real centimetres.
- Beside the mat, the stickers are five slots, top to bottom as on a gun. Click
  the chain between two slots to link them so they move and turn as one.
- Switch between the **Angled** view and **Top**, straight down for measuring
  (**V**).

Name it under **Craft name**, then **Save**, or **Update** the craft the mat
came from, or **Save as new**. **All crafts** shows every craft you've saved,
by gun and by search, with rename, publish and delete on each.

### Putting a craft on a gun

The button under the slots saves the craft and opens your equipped skin of that
gun with the craft placed ("Put on your M4A4"), or starts a plain one if you
have none equipped.

On any gun, **Your crafts** along the bottom of the item editor's 3D view lists
your crafts for that gun, each saying which slots it would take, or **Doesn't
fit**. One click puts a craft on as a group, which
moves and turns together until you **Ungroup** it. The sticker picker's
shelves are **Catalog**, **Owned**, **Your crafts** and **Craft Store**.

### Craft Store

A saved craft is **Private** until you publish it, which puts it **In store**
for everyone on the panel.

- Sort the store by **Top** or **New**. Each craft shows its author and its up
  and down votes: one vote per player, and none on your own.
- **Proposed use** shows the gun the author made it for and where it goes.
- **Place** opens a craft on your mat, to change and save as your own.

An administrator can take a craft down for good. Taking your own out of the
store and back keeps its votes.
