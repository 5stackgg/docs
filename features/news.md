# News

News gives your instance a front page, season announcements, rule changes,
tournament results, maintenance windows, written and published from inside
5Stack rather than lost in a Discord channel.

Enable it from **Settings → Application → News**.

## Reading

Players get a **News** section listing published articles with their cover image
and teaser, and can **mark as read** to clear the unread indicator. A minimum
role to view can be set, so you can publish internally to organizers before going
public.

## Writing

Article authoring is built in:

- **Markdown editor** with a live **Write / Preview** split, plus a full-post
  preview.
- **Cover image** upload (PNG / JPG / WEBP / GIF) with a crop tool and background
  removal, so logos sit cleanly on the page.
- **Inline image uploads** in the body.
- **Title** and **teaser**, the teaser is what shows in the news list.

## Drafts and publishing

Posts save as **drafts** first and publish when you're ready. Published posts can
be **unpublished** back to draft, edited, or deleted, and the manage screen shows
a **view count** per post.
