## What you must do

- Keep the **Powered by 5Stack** credit, with its link to
  [5stack.gg](https://5stack.gg), where the panel shows its legal notices:
  the **Licenses** page, linked from the sign-in page and the profile menu.

  network, offer them its source code under the AGPL.
- Don't present a changed version as the official 5Stack. The license gives

## Game Streamer

The [game streamer](/advanced/game-streaming/) image is free to run as
part of your own 5Stack installation, but it is not open source: you may not
modify it, resell it or host it as a service for others. Its HUD and Vulkan
capture components are GPL.

The streamer renders highlight intros and outros with
[Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev). Remotion is free for individuals,
non-profits and companies with up to three employees. A larger company
running the streamer needs a
[Remotion company license](https://www.remotion.dev/license).
